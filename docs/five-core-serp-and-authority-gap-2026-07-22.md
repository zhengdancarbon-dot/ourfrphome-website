# FRP HOME Five-Core SERP and Authority Gap

Date: 2026-07-22

Production: `https://www.myfrphome.com`

Scope: Multiaxial carbon NCF, 3K carbon fiber laminate sheet, carbon fiber yarn and tow, strengthening UD fabric and pultruded CFRP plate, and 3K woven carbon fiber fabric.

## Executive Finding

FRP HOME has the right technical foundation for qualified B2B search growth: stable product URLs, nine-language commercial pages, Product/FAQ/Breadcrumb schema, verified TDS documents, RFQ fields, compliance review language and source-aware conversion events. The immediate ranking gap is not missing keyword volume. It is the combination of low domain authority, limited search history, incomplete third-party industry citations and insufficient evidence for some order-specific values.

No title rewrite is justified solely by current rankings. The GSC samples are still small, and the 14-day or 50-impression rule remains active.

## Observed Result Patterns

Current search results for the five clusters repeatedly reward pages that make procurement decisions easy:

- Product architecture and orientation are visible near the top: UD, biaxial, triaxial, quadriaxial, `0°/90°` and `+45°/-45°`.
- Surface, machining and drawing requirements are explicit for 3K sheet: matte, glossy, thickness, CNC cutting, drilling and edge finishing.
- Tow count and downstream process intent are explicit for yarn and tow: weaving, braiding, prepreg, pultrusion, filament winding and chopping.
- Strengthening pages clearly separate UD fabric systems from pultruded CFRP plates and retain engineering review language.
- Buyers can find TDS, specification or document links without searching through a general catalog.
- RFQ prompts request the variables needed to quote rather than relying on generic contact copy.

FRP HOME now covers these patterns without copying unsupported competitor values.

## Search Cache and Domain Risks

### Stale Strengthening Snippet

Public search results may still display old generic numerical ranges for the strengthening page. The live page no longer contains those ranges, and the URL has already been submitted through IndexNow. Google URL Inspection should be used once after the seven-day recrawl window if the cached result remains stale.

### Old Domain Cannibalization

Search results still expose a 3K laminate page on `zdcarbonfiber.com` with wording that overlaps the current FRP HOME topic. No redirect or content removal should be attempted without a business ownership decision. The old domain should be reviewed for:

- common ownership and administrative control;
- continuing commercial purpose;
- canonical or redirect feasibility;
- overlapping titles, images and body copy;
- external links worth preserving.

Until that review is complete, FRP HOME should continue strengthening its own canonical page and verified documents rather than duplicating more copy.

## Outreach Rules

- Use a relevant product URL with a cluster-specific UTM campaign.
- Do not pitch PDF-only backlinks; the product page remains the primary landing page.
- Do not buy links, submit to mass directories or request exact-match anchor text.
- Provide an original technical contribution only when source material supports it.
- Record contact date, owner, reply and live citation URL.
- Perform destination, counterparty, final-use and applicable trade-compliance review before any Russia-related outreach.
- Do not mention or target military, weaponized drone, missile or combat uses.

## Measurement

Review weekly by cluster:

- indexed URL status and exclusion reason;
- non-brand impressions, clicks, CTR and average position;
- TDS downloads, RFQ, email and WhatsApp events by `source_page`;
- verified citations and referring pages;
- AI answer brand appearance, cited URL and factual accuracy;
- qualified replies and inquiries from tracked outreach.

Rankings are not guaranteed. The working objective is to move qualified commercial queries toward page one through evidence, crawlability, useful procurement content and legitimate industry authority.
