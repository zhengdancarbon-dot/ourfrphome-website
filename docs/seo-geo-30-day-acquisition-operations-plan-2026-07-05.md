## Objective

Move from launch validation to acquisition operations:

- Help Google and Bing discover the expanded 75 URL multilingual sitemap.
- Track which language and page groups generate traffic and RFQ behavior.
- Help salespeople use the website as a customer development tool.
- Use data to choose the next content and conversion improvements.

## Week 1 - Indexing and Baseline Data

Goal: confirm discovery, indexing and tracking.

Tasks:

- Resubmit `https://www.myfrphome.com/sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console.
- Resubmit `https://www.myfrphome.com/sitemap.xml` in Bing Webmaster Tools.
- Request indexing for priority EN, ES and PT-BR pages.
- Record baseline indexed pages, impressions, clicks, top queries and top pages.
- Confirm GA4 events:
  - `rfq_submit`
  - `whatsapp_click`
  - `email_click`
  - `catalog_download`
- Record RFQ submissions by locale.

Do not judge ranking performance yet. Week 1 is for discovery and baseline measurement.

## Week 2 - Strengthen Core Product Pages

Goal: improve pages that start receiving impressions or clicks.

Priority pages:

- `carbon-fiber-woven-fabric`
- `carbon-fiber-ud-fabric`
- `carbon-fiber-yarn-and-tow`
- `milled-carbon-fiber-powder`
- `prepreg-carbon-fiber-materials`
- `spread-tow-carbon-fiber-fabric`
- `structural-strengthening-system`

Optimization rules:

- If impressions are present but CTR is low, tune title and meta description only after data is visible.
- If clicks are present but RFQs are weak, strengthen CTA, RFQ checklist and application guidance.
- Add FAQ only when it answers real procurement questions.
- Do not add unverified certifications, authorization, capacity, test reports or customer cases.

## Week 3 - GEO / AI Search Content Enhancement

Goal: make pages easier for AI answer engines to understand and cite.

Allowed content components:

- Quick Answer
- Selection Table
- Comparison Table
- RFQ Checklist
- Recommended For / Not Recommended For
- FAQ

Priority answer prompts:

- What carbon fiber fabric should I use for vacuum infusion?
- Difference between UD carbon fiber fabric and woven carbon fiber fabric.
- What is the difference between chopped carbon fiber and milled carbon fiber powder?
- How to choose 3K twill carbon fiber fabric by GSM?
- What materials are used for CFRP structural strengthening?
- How to source carbon fiber yarn and tow from China?
- What information is needed for a custom carbon fiber part RFQ?

## Guardrails

- Keep English root URLs stable.
- Keep `/es/` and `/pt-br/` live.
- Keep `/en` unpublished. RU, AR, FR, KO, PL and TR enter indexing only after multilingual Preview QA and production approval.
- Do not modify sitemap, canonical or hreflang logic during the 14-day stability period unless there is a bug.
- Keep compliance and end-use review language active.
