Date: 2026-07-23

Status: active execution support for `six-core-exposure-growth-task-2026-07-23.md`

## Purpose

Build real industry discovery and referral traffic for FRP HOME through relevant composites associations, supplier directories, exhibitions and manually reviewed B2B platforms. This is not a bulk-link campaign. A placement is useful only when buyers can identify the company, understand the product scope and reach the correct commercial page.

The reviewed execution queue is maintained in `six-core-authority-outreach-shortlist-2026-07-23.csv`.

## Four-Week Execution

### Week 1

- Send eligibility and pricing questions to JEC, China Composites Expo and Composite-Expo.
- Prepare the company profile package and select one product landing page per channel.
- Record every contact date, reply and owner in the outreach CSV or sales CRM.

### Week 2

- Contact SAMPE China, AVK and ACMA.
- Start DirectIndustry and europages qualification only if company-name consistency can be maintained.
- Reject offers for paid followed links, automatic directory bundles or mass publication.

### Week 3

- Complete approved profiles with real product images, source-backed documents and UTM links.
- Request correction where a directory rewrites the company name, URL or product description inaccurately.
- Do not publish duplicate keyword pages to satisfy a directory form.

### Week 4

- Review referral sessions, product-page engagement, TDS downloads and RFQs by `utm_source` and campaign.
- Continue only channels producing relevant buyer visits, qualified contacts or defensible industry visibility.
- Mark no-response and poor-fit channels for a later quarterly review rather than repeated outreach.

## Acceptance Rules

- Every placement must use the production domain and a canonical product landing page.
- The external profile must not invent certifications, official authorization, annual capacity, test reports, customer cases, ratings or reviews.
- No military, weaponized drone, missile or combat-use wording is allowed.
- A directory listing is not counted as an SEO success until its public URL is verified and recorded.
- Referral performance is evaluated by relevant sessions, TDS downloads and qualified inquiries, not by the number of links created.
