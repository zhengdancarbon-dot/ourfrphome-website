# FRP HOME 3K Carbon Fiber Plate Priority-Market Promotion Plan

Date: 2026-08-04

Status: active market-execution addendum. This addendum takes priority for 3K
carbon fiber laminate-sheet promotion in the markets below; it does not replace
the existing site-wide SEO decision rules.

## Scope And Guardrails

- Product route: `/products/3k-carbon-fiber-laminate-sheet`.
- Product form: 3K visible-twill carbon fiber laminate sheet for panels and
  drawing-based CNC parts. It is not the pultruded CFRP strengthening plate.
- The English download is a Product Specification and RFQ Guide, not a
  batch-specific TDS. Do not represent it as verified mechanical-property data.
- Do not change a title or meta description without genuine GSC/Yandex query
  evidence and the existing 14-indexed-day or 50-impression decision rule.
- Do not publish unreviewed machine translation, country-only `hreflang`, or a
  language selector entry for a route that is not fully localized.
- All outreach requires a counterparty, destination, final-application and
  applicable trade-control review before contact. No automated directory
  submission, paid links or bulk email is permitted.

## Six-Week Execution

### Week 1: Measurement And Discovery

1. In Yandex Webmaster, add the two Russian URLs to saved-query monitoring and
   submit the production sitemap only if its status is not already successful.
2. In Google Search Console, filter Performance by each live localized URL,
   country and query. Record impressions, clicks, CTR, average position and
   indexed status without changing metadata.
3. Use the campaign URLs in
   `docs/3k-plate-priority-market-utm-pack-2026-08-04.csv` for manually
   approved outreach. Sales must record organization, contact, source URL,
   country, application, destination and result.
4. Check that the current Russian, Polish and Korean pages show the English
   specification/RFQ guide accurately and preserve the end-use review.

### Weeks 2-3: Russia First

1. Monitor the Russian 3K plate and NCF query groups weekly in Yandex
   Webmaster. Use Yandex data as the decision source for Russian wording; do
   not copy terms into metadata merely because they appear in a planning file.
2. Prepare two source-safe, Russian-language outreach briefs: one for 3K
   laminate-sheet drawing/CNC RFQs and one for 300gsm/600gsm NCF procurement.
3. Review the existing Russia/CIS distributor and event prospects in
   `docs/five-core-authority-outreach-tracker-2026-07-22.csv`. Contact only
   prospects that pass business and compliance review.
4. For any qualified inquiry, capture plate dimensions, thickness, finish,
   drawing, quantity and final application; for NCF capture architecture,
   fiber directions, gsm, width, roll length, resin process and end use.

### Weeks 3-4: Poland And South Korea

1. Use the existing `/pl/` and `/ko/` product pages for relevant supplier and
   drawing-based CNC inquiries; no duplicate country page is needed.
2. Test Polish and Korean RFQ submission, email delivery, locale and source
   URL once per release, not repeatedly in a burst.
3. South Korea: submit only the production sitemap and the Korean 3K page to
   Naver Search Advisor after ownership access is provided. Do not create a
   Naver blog or listing with unverified claims.

### Weeks 4-6: New-Language Readiness

1. Commission or obtain a native review for Ukrainian, Vietnamese and Thai
   product, RFQ, FAQ and compliance copy. Technical strings remain unchanged:
   `3K`, `CFRP`, `CNC`, `DXF`, `STEP` and numeric dimensions.
2. Publish a new locale only as a coherent set: product page, contact/RFQ,
   navigation labels, metadata, self-canonical, reciprocal hreflang, sitemap
   entry, structured data, mobile QA and email locale/source-url verification.
3. Do not add `uk`, `vi` or `th` to the language switcher, sitemap or
   hreflang until that QA is complete. Google requires localized alternates to
   link to themselves and every available counterpart, including `x-default`.
4. Once live, use one URL Inspection / Request Indexing action for each new
   localized product page, then let sitemap discovery work rather than
   repeating submissions.

## Weekly Scorecard

Report every market separately:

- indexed state and sitemap / crawl issue, if any;
- non-brand impressions, clicks, CTR and position for the target URLs;
- top queries and country share;
- RFQ, specification-download, email and WhatsApp actions with locale and UTM;
- qualified inquiries, destination/end-use review status and next owner action;
- verified industry citations or partner listings; and
- next test only after the 14-day / 50-impression rule is met.

## External-Platform Constraints

- Yandex Webmaster is already the working Russian search channel; its data is
  the source of truth for Yandex changes.
- Google handles the other current live language pages. Use language versions,
  not a country code alone, for international targeting.
- Naver Search Advisor requires an owner session before sitemap submission;
  no account action is assumed in this plan.
- Ukraine, Vietnam and Thailand have no current local-language FRP HOME route,
  so their immediate activity is approved English B2B outreach, not a false
  localized SEO promise.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Russia has separate, measured 3K plate and NCF query groups in Yandex.
2. Poland and South Korea use the existing localized 3K pages with attributed
   links and functioning RFQ paths.
3. Ukraine, Vietnam and Thailand receive no misleading language routing.
4. Any newly published locale passes canonical, hreflang, schema, mobile and
   RFQ locale/source-url QA before search submission.
5. No activity introduces unsupported technical values, certifications,
   authorization, customer cases or sensitive end-use language.
