# FRP HOME Technical Content Backlog

Date: 2026-07-15
Goal: 30 buyer-useful technical resources, released through quality gates rather than mass publication.

## Publication Standard

Every new article must include:

- A specific buyer or engineer question and a direct Quick Answer.
- Original selection logic, comparison criteria or RFQ guidance.
- A clear distinction between typical sourcing information and confirmed order data.
- Source review for any material property, handling, cure, safety or engineering claim.
- Authoring organization, review date, Article schema, FAQ schema and breadcrumbs.
- Relevant internal links without repeating the same keyword unnaturally.
- No unsupported certification, authorization, capacity, test report or customer case.
- No military, weaponized drone, missile or combat UAV wording.

Google does not recommend writing to an arbitrary word count. Most resources should be as long as needed to answer the purchase decision, typically 1,200-2,200 words after technical review; a shorter complete answer is preferable to padded text.

## 30-Day Release Gate

The backlog contains 30 total resources, but publication is gated:

1. Review the 10 existing articles for accuracy, authorship and internal links.
2. Publish no more than two fully reviewed new resources per week during the first month.
3. Keep the remaining planned resources as briefs until product documents or a knowledgeable reviewer supports the claims.
4. Request indexing only after the article is linked from the Technical Center and its target pillar page.
5. After 14 days or 50 impressions, use GSC query data to decide whether the title/meta needs refinement.

This pace protects topical quality while maintaining a ready 30-topic editorial pipeline.
