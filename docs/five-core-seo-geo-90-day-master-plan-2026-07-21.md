## Cluster 1: Multiaxial Carbon NCF

Primary page: `/products/carbon-fiber-multiaxial-ncf-fabric`

Target 300gsm and 600gsm biaxial, `+45°/-45°`, `0°/90°`, triaxial, quadriaxial, vacuum infusion, RTM and custom-width procurement intent. Retain the two verified FRP HOME TDS files. Generic ranges are not order guarantees.

## Cluster 2: 3K Carbon Fiber Laminate Sheet

Primary page: `/products/3k-carbon-fiber-laminate-sheet`

Target 3K twill sheet, matte/glossy plate, custom thickness, CNC cutting, drilling and drawing-based quotation intent. Keep this product distinct from pultruded structural-strengthening CFRP plate. The current PDF remains a Product Specification & RFQ Guide, not a formal product TDS.

## Cluster 3: Carbon Fiber Yarn & Tow

Primary page: `/products/carbon-fiber-yarn-and-tow`

Target carbon fiber tow supplier, yarn supplier, 1K-50K tow, T300/T700/T800, weaving, braiding, pultrusion, filament winding and prepreg-conversion intent. Ask for tow size, grade, sizing, package, process, quantity, destination and final application. Keep the verified 12K Tow TDS visible. No official brand authorization or stock guarantee is implied.

## Cluster 4: Structural Strengthening

Primary commercial pages:

- `/products/carbon-fiber-ud-fabric` for general UD fabric, 200/300/600gsm and narrow-width searches
- `/products/structural-strengthening-system` for strengthening UD fabric, pultruded CFRP plate and structural epoxy system searches

Query routing must prevent cannibalization:

- Generic `UD carbon fiber fabric supplier` searches map to the UD product page.
- `carbon fiber fabric for concrete strengthening`, `pultruded CFRP plate` and system queries map to the strengthening-system page.
- Structural design, substrate evaluation and adhesive approval remain the responsibility of qualified project engineers.

The verified 300gsm UD Carbon Fiber Fabric TDS may appear on both relevant product pages. A pultruded strengthening-plate TDS requires matching source records and must not be inferred from the 3K laminate guide.

## Cluster 5: 3K Woven Carbon Fiber Fabric

Primary page: `/products/carbon-fiber-woven-fabric`

Target 3K woven carbon fabric, 3K twill cloth, 200gsm carbon fabric, plain weave, supplier and custom-width searches. Preserve the verified 3K 200gsm Twill TDS. The page may also describe 1K, 6K and 12K options, but 3K 200gsm twill remains the leading commercial intent.

## Day 1-30: Commercial Foundation

1. Give all five clusters prominent contextual links from Home, Products and Technical Center.
2. Keep sitemap, canonical, hreflang, Schema and RFQ routing stable.
3. Validate `rfq_submit`, `tds_download`, `catalog_download`, `email_click` and `whatsapp_click` with locale and product context.
4. Use GSC query evidence to refine only pages that reach 50 impressions or 14 indexed days.
5. Publish or update, after technical review:
   - 300gsm vs 600gsm Biaxial Carbon NCF Selection Guide
   - Carbon Fiber Tow Size Guide: 1K-50K
   - 3K 200gsm Woven Carbon Fabric Selection and RFQ Guide
   - CFRP Plate vs UD Fabric for Structural Strengthening
   - 3K Carbon Fiber Sheet CNC and RFQ Guide
6. Start manually reviewed outreach with cluster-specific UTM campaigns.

## Day 31-60: Authority And Localization

- Strengthen each cluster with one source-backed comparison, selection or RFQ resource rather than thin keyword pages.
- Review localized commercial terminology, CTA, RFQ and document labels in all eight non-English locales.
- Link articles to the correct product page, download and RFQ; do not publish orphan pages.
- Test EN, ES, PT-BR, RU, FR and TR procurement questions in verifiable AI search interfaces.
- Contact relevant composites directories, engineering media, distributors, processors and real partners only after manual review.

## Day 61-90: Ranking And Conversion

- Prioritize queries in positions 11-20 with answer depth, specifications and contextual internal links before further title experiments.
- Audit pages after 20 valid organic visits without RFQ, TDS or contact action.
- Compare query growth, country mix, document downloads, qualified RFQs and AI citations by cluster.
- Update title/meta only from real query evidence and preserve URLs and slugs.

## Decision Rules

- Not indexed after seven days: perform one live URL test and one indexing request.
- Indexed for 14 days or 50 impressions: review query-to-title alignment once.
- 20 valid organic visits without action: inspect CTA, RFQ and document visibility.
- Do not create a separate page for every GSM, tow size, thickness or synonym without distinct source-backed content and search intent.
- Do not invent search volume, ranking, technical values, certification, authorization, annual capacity, customer cases or test conclusions.
- Do not add military, weaponized drone, missile or combat-use wording.

## Weekly Measurement

Report each cluster separately:

- indexed status and exclusion reason
- non-brand impressions, clicks, CTR and average position
- top queries, countries, locales and landing pages
- TDS/SPEC downloads, RFQs, WhatsApp, email and catalog actions
- source URL, UTM, inquiry specification, status and owner
- verified backlinks, citations and AI answer accuracy

Every release must pass `pnpm lint`, `pnpm build`, desktop/mobile checks, canonical/hreflang validation and production smoke tests.
