# FRP HOME Phase C External Authority Plan

Status: research and preparation only. This file does not authorize submissions, payments, account creation or statements of partnership.

## Candidate types

| Type | Purpose | Evidence required before outreach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Composite-material directory | Supplier discovery and company citation | Relevant category, real editorial review, no pay-to-rank claim |
| Association or exhibition profile | Verifiable industry presence | Actual membership, exhibitor record or organizer permission |
| Distributor or partner page | Commercial trust and referral traffic | Written partner approval and accurate product scope |
| Trade publication contribution | Technical visibility | Article supported by approved product documents and editorial review |

## Screening rules

1. Keep only sites with a clear owner, relevant audience and accessible editorial/contact path.
2. Reject automated listing packages, link farms, unrelated consumer directories and guarantees of rankings.
3. Use the existing canonical product page as the destination, not a PDF or a country doorway page.
4. Use a documented UTM only for manual outreach. Never place personal data in the UTM.
5. Do not claim certifications, authorizations, capacity, customer relationships, project outcomes or sensitive end uses without real supporting documents.

## Monthly operating target

- Research up to 15 candidates.
- Qualify up to 5 candidates using the screening rules.
- Draft truthful outreach for human approval.
- Record only approved, completed references and resulting referral/RFQ evidence in the weekly SEO report.

1. Multiaxial NCF: composite manufacturing and infusion/RTM audiences.
2. 3K Carbon Fiber Laminate Sheet: industrial plate/CNC procurement audiences.
3. Woven Carbon Fiber Fabric: reinforcement-material supplier audiences.
4. UD and Structural Strengthening: construction-material channels only where compliance language remains intact.
