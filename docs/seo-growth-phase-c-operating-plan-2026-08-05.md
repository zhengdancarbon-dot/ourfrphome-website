## Evidence sources and boundaries

- GSC is the source of truth for Google clicks, impressions, CTR, position, query, page and country.
- GA4 is the source of truth for on-site event counts. Track `rfq_submit`, `tds_download`, `catalog_download`, `whatsapp_click` and `email_click` without personal data.
- Yandex Webmaster or Metrica supplies Yandex impressions, clicks, ranking and country where exported.
- Sales data contains only de-identified, aggregate qualified-inquiry records and never contact details or message content.
- Manual AI-search checks and external-authority activity are evidence only after their results are recorded and reviewed.

## Decision rules

1. Before 14 days of evidence or 50 GSC impressions: observe only. Do not change metadata.
2. Position 11-30: prepare an answer-quality and contextual internal-link proposal. Do not auto-publish it.
3. At least 50 impressions with CTR below 1%: prepare a query-led title/meta proposal for human approval.
4. At least 20 GA4 page views with no RFQ, TDS, email or WhatsApp action: prepare a conversion review for CTA prominence, RFQ fields and document visibility.
5. Do not treat rankings, clicks, AI citations or sales quality as proven without an exported or reviewed source record.

## External authority preparation

Prioritize manual, relevant references from composite-material directories, genuine distributors or partners, association/exhibitor records and relevant trade publications. Each candidate must be checked for relevance, editorial quality, truthful company information, geographic fit and compliance language. Do not buy links, use automated directory submissions or represent unverified affiliations.
