# FRP HOME Page-One Ranking Execution

Date: 2026-07-18

Production domain: https://www.myfrphome.com

## Objective

The business objective is first-page visibility for qualified searches related to carbon multiaxial NCF and 3K carbon fiber laminate sheet. No ethical SEO process can guarantee a fixed Google position, so execution is measured by index coverage, non-brand impressions, top-20 movement, qualified document downloads and RFQs.

## Keyword Coverage

- Existing broad library: 127 mapped keywords.
- New dual-core library: 120 mapped keywords.
- Combined working set: 247 keywords without invented search-volume figures.
- NCF allocation: 60 keywords.
- 3K plate allocation: 60 keywords.

The new library separates supplier terms, architecture, orientation, gsm, width, process, finish, thickness, CNC and RFQ intent. Similar terms map to one authoritative page instead of creating duplicate landing pages.

## Ranking Sequence

1. Win long-tail specification searches such as `600gsm +45 -45 carbon NCF` and `2mm 3K carbon fiber plate`.
2. Build relevance for mid-tail commercial searches such as `biaxial carbon fiber fabric supplier` and `CNC carbon fiber plate supplier`.
3. Compete for broad terms such as `carbon fiber multiaxial fabric` and `3K carbon fiber plate` after the product clusters earn impressions, links and engagement.

## On-Page Changes

- NCF metadata now prioritizes supplier, 300gsm, 600gsm, +45°/-45°, 0°/90°, infusion, RTM and custom width intent.
- 3K plate metadata now prioritizes plate/sheet supplier, 0.5-20 mm typical range, matte/glossy, custom size and CNC-to-drawing intent.
- Both pages now answer high-intent RFQ questions in visible FAQ content and FAQPage schema.
- Selection guidance requests the exact details needed to qualify a real inquiry.

## Measurement

Track the two product clusters separately in GSC and GA4:

- indexed URL status
- non-brand impressions and clicks
- query position bands: 1-3, 4-10, 11-20, 21-50, 51+
- `tds_download`, `rfq_submit`, `email_click`, `whatsapp_click`
- source URL, locale, country and qualified inquiry status

Review metadata only after a page has been indexed for 14 days or reaches 50 impressions. A query in positions 11-20 receives internal-link and answer-depth work before title experimentation.

## Boundaries

- Keep all existing URLs and slugs stable.
- Do not create one page per keyword variation.
- Do not publish unsupported certification, capacity, customer, performance or tolerance claims.
- Do not purchase links, mass-submit directories or use search-result scraping as fabricated demand data.
- Technical values remain subject to the published document scope, quotation and order confirmation.
